Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

In short

Oh My Art! is an app to learn art history that is not out yet. For now, this site does one thing with personal data: if you leave your email address, we keep it to send you one email when the app is out, and then we delete it. There are no ads, no cookies and no tracking on this site, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Oh My Art! is published by Sheyenne Hervin EI, an individual entrepreneur registered in France under SIREN 981 390 495, whose registered address is 60 rue François 1er, 75008 Paris, France. We are responsible for your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach us at hello@ohmyart.app.

If you join the waitlist

We keep:

  • your email address,
  • the date and time you joined,
  • which form you used and the reference of the link you followed if it had one, such as the video you came from.

Why: to send you one email when Oh My Art! is out. Nothing else: no newsletter, no offers. We rely on your consent, which you give by sending the form, and you can take it back at any time by writing to us.

Where: the list is stored with Supabase, on servers in London, United Kingdom, a country the European Union recognises as protecting personal data at the same level. Nobody can read the list from the website: only we can, from our Supabase account.

How long: until the launch email is sent. Then we delete the list. If the app never comes out, we delete it at the latest on December 31, 2027.

When you write to us

Your email address and the content of your message, to answer you. Emails are handled by Zoho Mail, on servers in the European Union. We keep the conversation for as long as we need it to help you, and no longer than 3 years after your last message.

When you visit the site

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser asks for a page, to deliver it and keep the service secure, and may keep it in its logs for a limited time. We do not see these logs and we use no analytics. The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other websites: even its fonts are served from ohmyart.app.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can take back your consent to the waitlist. Write to hello@ohmyart.app and we will answer within one month. If you think we are not handling your data properly, you can file a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or the authority of the country you live in.

Changes

When the app comes out, it will have its own, longer privacy policy, and this page will be updated. The date at the top shows the last change.
